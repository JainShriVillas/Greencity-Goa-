# Greencity Goa — Project Brief

## What this is
A single-page, conversion-optimized, search-indexable landing page for Greencity Goa (by Jain Shri Villas), replacing the earlier "The Moon's Path" brand. One scrolling page, section-anchored — not a multi-page site, not a gated ad-only landing page. It also functions as a trust-building/trust-reinforcing touchpoint for anyone researching the project on their own, and as the conversion location itself whenever Meta or Google Ads campaigns are run pointing directly at this page.

## Goal
Three jobs, in priority order:
1. **Findability** — rank for branded search ("Greencity Goa", "Jain Shri Villas Goa") so warm audiences who saw a hoarding or social ad can find and convert later.
2. **Conversion** — turn paid traffic (Meta + Google Ads) into qualified leads via a fast, low-friction path.
3. **Credibility** — read as a premium, real, already-delivering developer, not a rendering-only pre-launch page.

## Audience
- Warm/retargeted visitors: saw a hoarding, Instagram ad, or word-of-mouth mention, then searched the brand name directly.
- Cold paid traffic: Meta/Google prospecting audiences interested in Goa second homes / land investment.
- Likely buyer profiles: NRI/investor buyers, second-home buyers, local upgraders — *confirm actual buyer mix with client, don't assume*.

## Governing belief (everything on the page pays off this one idea)
**"North Goa is about to change forever — Greencity Goa lets you own it before that happens."**

Backed by the project's own "Next Global Growth Destination" content (Taj resort, casino zone, cricket stadium, airport, film city, IT hub) — this is the proof, not just a claim.

## Tone / voice
Belief-led, not feature-led — introduce a way of thinking, then let specs become proof of that belief (Panda/Viztara framework: idea → emotion → proof → features → CTA). Conversational, like explaining over coffee — not corporate, not overwrought poetic copy.

## Reference points
- **Competitor/UX reference:** promenadevillas.in ("The Promenade," Kollur, Hyderabad) — strong hero+form pattern, belief-led section headers, multiple CTA touchpoints. See earlier critique notes for what to copy vs. improve.
- **Visual/structural reference:** `greencity-goa-landing-mockup.html` — the built wireframe is the concrete target. Match this layout and section flow unless content.md says otherwise.
- **Source material:** original "Moon's Path" brochure (JSV) — factual base for location, amenities, investment case; brochure is being remade in parallel, so treat brochure facts as provisional pending final sign-off.

## Success
See `Goals and Success Framework` doc. Short version: qualified leads at an acceptable cost, branded search ownership, and a site that doesn't need touching again for months — not vanity traffic.
